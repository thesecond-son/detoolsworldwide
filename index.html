Who we are

An indigenous Nigerian firm built on field-tested competence.

DE Tools Worldwide Limited is an engineering, procurement and construction company incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act and headquartered at No. 125 Aggrey Road, Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

We were founded to maximise local content in the oil and gas industry, pairing experienced, accredited engineers and field technicians with the equipment and procurement networks needed to execute civil, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation works for parastatals, multinational operators and private firms across the Niger Delta and beyond.

Our vision

To become a formidable local and international player in the Engineering Services & Procurement sector, drawing primarily on Nigerian talent and resources, and to lead the African engineering market.

  • Comprehensive, economically engineered solutions for every client requirement.
  • A safe, supportive work environment that enables personnel development.
  • Eco-friendly processes and active development programmes for our host communities.

"Our edge is simple: Nigerian engineers, Nigerian grit, and a refusal to cut corners on safety or quality, on any site, at any scale."

Civil, Mechanical & E/I Engineering

Tag · ACC

Access Solutions & Rope Access

Complex, suspended, cantilever and overboard scaffolding, plus IRATA-trained rope access teams for technicians, fitters, riggers and painters working at height.

Tag · COAT

Protective Coating & Blasting

Anti-corrosion and specialty coatings, metal spraying, plus grit and hydro blasting and surface preparation for marine, pipe and structural steel.

Tag · INS

Insulation & Passive Fire-Proofing

Thermal, cryogenic, acoustic and anti-condensation insulation, plus intumescent and cementitious fireproofing for steel and concrete structures.

Tag · RPR

Composite Pipe Repair

Engineered composite wrap and cold-bonding repair systems for external/internal corrosion, live leaks and through-wall pipeline defects.

Tag · NDT

Inspection & NDT

Ultrasonic thickness measurement, pipeline leak/pressure/fitness testing, visual, dye penetrant, magnetic particle and radiographic testing.

Safety & assurance

Safety is a signed commitment, not a slogan.

Every scope we run sits under five written, MD-signed policies covering Safety, Health, Security, Environment and Community Affairs backed by a 12 Life-Saving Rules framework that governs permits, isolation, confined-space entry, gas testing and work at height.

12 Life-Saving Rules
Applied to every permit-to-work
Covers, among others
  • Valid work permits
  • Gas testing on entry
  • Isolation verification
  • Confined-space authorisation
  • Fall protection at height
  • Journey management plans
