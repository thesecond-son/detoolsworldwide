Who we are

An engineering firm built on field-tested competence.

DE Tools Worldwide Limited is an indigenous Nigerian engineering, procurement and construction firm, incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act and headquartered at No. 125 Aggrey Road, Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

We were set up to promote local content in the oil and gas industry, and we lean heavily on the depth of our engineers and field technicians — over 23 certified professionals carrying real track records into every job we take on, from pre-construction through to completion.

That combination of fresh strategic direction and seasoned engineering hands is what lets us move from design straight through to execution — civil, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation — for parastatals, multinational operators, and private firms across the Niger Delta and beyond.

What drives us

Vision, mission and the culture behind the work.

Our vision

To become a formidable local and international player in the Engineering Services & Procurement sector — drawing primarily on Nigerian talent and resources — and to lead the African engineering market.

  • Comprehensive, economically engineered solutions for every client requirement.
  • A safe, pleasant work environment that supports personnel development and advancement.
  • Eco-friendly processes and development programmes for our host communities.

Our people are safety-conscious, energetic and result-oriented — and we invest in keeping them that way, with regular health screening, ongoing safety training, and a career-development programme that runs throughout their time with us. Every project is treated on its own terms, and every team shares one principle: do the job right the first time.

Safety & assurance

Five signed policies. One standard, on every job.

Safety, Health, Security, Environment and Community Affairs are each governed by a written policy signed by our Managing Director — backed by a 12 Life-Saving Rules framework covering permits, isolation, gas testing, confined space and work at height.
